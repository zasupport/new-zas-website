# Ideal State

The evidence-led end state the programme builds toward. Not a description of today; a target to
close the gap against. Per handoff §22.

## The platform in its ideal state

A cost-conscious, local-first, evidence-led and self-improving website that:

1. **Preserves the iPhone acquisition engine.** High-volume iPhone repair pages, URLs, traffic and
   conversion flow are measured and protected; changed only on measured evidence, never on a hunch.
2. **Makes Mac repair a first-class pillar.** Screen, battery, keyboard, trackpad, charging /
   no-power, logic-board, liquid-damage, data recovery, iMac and Mac mini each have a genuinely
   useful page with real workshop experience, resolving cannibalisation against the homepage, hubs
   and blog.
3. **Captures lawful high-intent security and forensic demand.** Consumer assessment, SME incident
   triage, M365 compromise response and attorney-directed evidence work, each behind verified
   capability, legal, privacy and safety gates, with no recovery or bypass claims.
4. **Tells the truth, consistently.** One source for reviews (GBP), one approved figure for each
   trust claim, "Established in 2009", the three repair metrics kept separate, and the assessment
   fee stated identically everywhere (R599 mobile, R899 computers, separate and non-refundable).
5. **Converts, not just ranks.** Every high-value page moves a qualified visitor toward an
   assessment booking. Ranking and clicks are guardrails; completed jobs and margin are the goal.
6. **Measures every change.** A commit links to a deployment, to affected routes, to GSC / GBP /
   GA4 movement, to assessment / quote / completed-job / margin, to a keep-or-rollback decision, to
   a reusable lesson. Nothing is asserted that was not measured.
7. **Improves itself.** Perplexity research, Claude.ai planning, Claude Code implementation, local
   tooling and operational decisions form one governed learning loop, with independent verification
   at every transition and no self-certified "done".

## What "not ideal" looks like (the failure modes to design out)

- A content farm of thin service x location x model permutations (doorway risk).
- Trust claims that conflict across pages (three different review counts; fee "credited" on one
  page and "separate" on another).
- Ranking optimised in isolation from conversion and margin.
- Security pages that promise recovery, bypass or covert monitoring, or claim court admissibility.
- Changes shipped without evidence, test, approval, rollback or a recorded lesson.
- A capability declared unavailable without being checked.

## How progress is judged

Against the acceptance criteria in handoff §21 (search, technical SEO, commercial policy, organic
structure, GBP/history, security/forensics, governance) and, above all, against the primary success
measure: qualified leads through to completed-job gross margin.
